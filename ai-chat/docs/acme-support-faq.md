# Acme Gadgets — Support FAQ

## Returns

Acme Gadgets accepts returns within 45 days of purchase, as long as the item
is in its original packaging. Refunds are issued to the original payment
method within 5 business days of the return being received.

## Support hours

Acme's customer support team is available Monday through Friday, 8am to
7pm Eastern Time. Support is closed on major US holidays.

## Warranty

Every Acme Gadgets product includes a 2-year limited warranty covering
manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage or
normal wear and tear. To file a warranty claim, contact support with your
order number.

## Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Acme also offers expedited
shipping, which arrives in 1 to 2 business days for an additional fee.
